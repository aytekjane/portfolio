HOROLOGY: UNIMATIC UC1 SKELETON CONCEPT

A skeleton variation of the Modello Uno UC1 Classic diver watch was created for Unimatic using an NH72 movement, with a transparent dial designed to reveal the mechanism while preserving the watch’s original minimalist character through a circular screen-printed pattern that creates a subtle semi-transparent effect; the movement is also visible through a window on the case back, while gray-toned corporate typography on the bezel indicates the seconds, and the transparency of both the front and back responds to the curiosity of users new to automatic watches, who often want to observe the mechanical movement, especially in their first timepiece.

HOROLOGY: CITIZEN CONCEPT

This project is based on Citizen’s Eco-Drive technology, which is powered by light energy, and features a concept watch with a clean interface displaying day, battery level, and date information, reflecting the brand’s commitment to sustainable and high-tech watchmaking; the design is inspired by the cherry blossom (sakura) of Japan, incorporating its delicate and minimal aesthetic into the overall concept.

INTERIOR: VAKKORAMA STORE PARAMETRIC LIGHTING INSTALLATION (CLIENT: VAKKORAMA)

A study for the Vakkorama store at Zorlu Center in Istanbul. The project incorporates parametric lighting for the vertical walls. It was developed with the intention of having minimal impact on the existing lighting scheme created by lighting designer Ali Berkman, which defines the spatial light map. The light leaking from the frames was controlled within a defined mapping system, where the scale of the circular pattern on the printed surface was parametrically adjusted through software.

Photography: Ali Berkman, Aytek Jane VAKKORAMA.COM.TR

INTERIOR: SAMSUNG EMAAR MALL - EXPERIENCE STORE (IN-HOUSE WORK: PROJECT MANAGER - ONERX)

Interior design and project management for the Samsung Experience Store at Emaar Mall were carried out as a collaborative team effort involving project managers, designers, manufacturers, and contractors. The project focused on retail flow and technological integration, developed in accordance with the design guidelines created by Simon Orchover, former retail design director at Samsung and Apple. Recognized as a world-first concept, the Samsung Experience Store combines experience, sales, and after-sales services within a single unified environment. As Samsung expands this model globally, the Istanbul store has been regarded as a reference point. Within the store, first-class technical services are provided to ensure customers can continuously benefit from Samsung technologies without interruption.
